Dina Privacy Policy Last updated: October 23, 2026 Dina (ದಿನ) is a simple daily task planner for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Dina does not collect your data. Information we collect None. Dina does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your data. Your tasks The tasks and schedules you create are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, and the developer cannot see them. Dina does not use iCloud or any other cloud sync. If you delete the app, its data on your device is deleted with it. Analytics, advertising, and tracking Dina contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites. Notifications If the app offers reminders and you allow notifications, they are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. No information is sent to us to make this work. Children's privacy Because Dina collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.
